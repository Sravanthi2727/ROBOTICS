--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. QUICKEST WAY: RUN ALL 5 ASSIGNMENTS AT ONCE (PYTHON)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a terminal and run:
  cd ~/GAZEBO/LAB11
  python3 run_all.py

What this does:
  - Executes Assignment 1 through Assignment 5 sequentially.
  - Outputs the simulation trajectory and convergence statistics in terminal.
  - Generates high-resolution figures for each assignment inside ~/GAZEBO/LAB11/plots/
  - Generates the master comparison figure: plots/lab11_master_overview.png
  - Prints a comprehensive performance metric table.
